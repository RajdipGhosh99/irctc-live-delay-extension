1. Individual & Non-Commercial Use

This extension is strictly for interactive individual passenger lookups. Automated commercial resale and bulk scraping are prohibited.

2. Anti-Abuse & Rate Compliance

Automated crawler loops, mass queries, and denial-of-service traffic are strictly prevented to protect upstream networks.

3. Independent Project

This is an independent open-source tool not affiliated with or endorsed by any official train booking portals.

4. Informational Estimates

Status data and delay predictions are public estimates. Always verify official display boards at railway stations before traveling.
